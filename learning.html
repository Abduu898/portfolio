Challenges & Failures

A recurring challenge is separating a visual symptom from the rule that caused it. While building this site, the Talk menu opened partly off-screen on smaller layouts. I traced it to a CSS selector that assumed the dropdown was the first tab, then corrected the alignment and limited its width on narrow screens.

I also found that the contact form originally showed a success message without actually sending anything. The form now validates the fields and opens a prefilled email draft, so its feedback describes what really happens. These fixes reinforced a useful routine: reproduce the behavior, inspect the markup and styles, make one focused change, and verify the result.
