Context & Objective

These projects were explored through Enactus as collaborative product initiatives. They provided experience in turning ideas into clearer user needs, defining useful features, and working with teammates to shape practical digital services.

Architecture & Technologies

FindNgo was developed as a car-rental platform. The work focused on platform architecture, user flows between vehicle owners and renters, and the teamwork required to shape a clear marketplace experience.

PeelWork was developed as a job board. The work focused on user workflow design, feature planning, and the structure of a web application connecting job seekers with relevant opportunities.

Challenges & Key Learnings

A key challenge was turning broad product ideas into clear, testable scopes. For FindNgo, this meant thinking through the needs of both sides of a car-rental platform. For PeelWork, it meant prioritizing job-search workflows and useful features for candidates and employers.

The experience strengthened my product thinking, user-flow analysis, and ability to contribute within a team. It also showed how platform structure and feature decisions influence whether a web application feels clear and useful.
