Context & Objective

This work combines practical e-commerce experiments with digital content projects. The objective has been to learn how a product or idea is presented online, how people find it, and how a project can be improved using feedback and observed results.

Architecture & Technologies

Storefront work has included Shopify and WordPress, with attention to product pages, site setup, and customer experience. Content work has involved short-form planning, editing workflows, and organic distribution across social platforms.

Challenges & Key Learnings

Online projects require more than publishing a page: presentation, navigation, audience, and iteration all affect whether the experience works. I learned to treat content and storefront changes as experiments, pay attention to what users respond to, and improve the next iteration rather than relying only on assumptions.

Social content projects have accumulated more than 55 million views. This is a combined audience metric across projects, not a result attributed to a single storefront.
